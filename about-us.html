About HR Fram

Making agricultural decisions more informed and accessible.

HR Fram is an AI-based smart crop recommendation and yield prediction system designed to help farmers make data-driven agricultural decisions through a simple web platform. The project combines crop recommendation, yield estimation, environmental information and optional soil data without requiring expensive farm hardware.

The problem we are addressing

Reducing uncertainty in crop selection and production planning.

Agricultural decisions are often influenced by experience, changing environmental conditions and limited access to analytical tools. HR Fram is being developed to make useful agricultural analysis easier to access through a software-based system.

The challenge

Farmers may rely on traditional experience and guesswork when selecting crops and estimating production. Environmental uncertainty can increase the risk of unsuitable crop choices and financial loss.

Our approach

HR Fram brings environmental information, optional soil-test values, machine learning and a web dashboard together so users can receive crop recommendations and estimated yield information in one connected workflow.

What HR Fram provides

A practical decision-support platform built around real farm inputs.

The project is intentionally focused on understandable, useful features rather than expensive hardware or unnecessarily complex interaction.

01

Crop Recommendation

Machine-learning-based crop suggestions using available environmental and soil information.

02

Yield Prediction

Estimated crop yield and production output to support better planning before harvest.

03

Automatic Weather

Environmental values can be retrieved from the user's selected location instead of requiring manual entry every time.

04

Optional Soil Inputs

Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and pH values can be included when soil-test information is available.

Project direction

Built to remain practical, affordable and understandable.

PRACTICAL

Farmer-friendly by design

The interface focuses on simple forms, clear results and familiar agricultural information instead of technical AI terminology.

ACCESSIBLE

No special hardware required

HR Fram is designed as a software-based solution, avoiding the cost and maintenance requirements of physical IoT installations.

CONNECTED

One intelligent dashboard

Crop recommendation and yield prediction are connected so a user can move from choosing a crop directly into planning expected production.
