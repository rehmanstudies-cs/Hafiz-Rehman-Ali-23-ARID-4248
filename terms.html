Terms of Use

Terms for using the HR Fram prototype.

These terms describe the intended use of the academic prototype. They are a project-stage draft and should receive formal legal review before public production use.

HR Fram is currently an academic decision-support prototype. Model outputs and demo results must not be treated as guaranteed agricultural outcomes.
Purpose Accounts Agricultural Guidance Acceptable Use Availability Changes

1. Purpose of HR Fram

HR Fram is being developed as an AI-based agricultural decision-support system for crop recommendation and yield prediction. The current version is a front-end academic prototype and some outputs are demonstration values.

2. Accounts and access

Users are responsible for the information they enter into the system. The current dummy login account is provided only for prototype testing. Real account security, password hashing, role-based access and database authentication will be implemented in the backend stage.

3. Agricultural guidance and limitations

Recommendations and yield estimates are intended to support decision-making, not guarantee crop performance or financial return. Agricultural outcomes can be affected by weather, soil, irrigation, seed variety, pests, disease, management practices and other factors.

Users should consider appropriate professional or local agricultural advice when making important farming decisions.

4. Acceptable use

Users should not:

  • attempt to interfere with or damage the service;
  • upload malicious or intentionally misleading files;
  • attempt unauthorized access to administrative features or other accounts;
  • present prototype demonstration results as guaranteed or officially certified agricultural advice.

5. Availability and accuracy

Because HR Fram is still under development, features may change, become unavailable or produce incomplete results. Weather services, datasets and machine-learning models may also have limitations or temporary failures.

6. Future changes

These terms should be updated before production deployment and whenever material changes are made to authentication, data handling, model functionality, hosting or external integrations.
