Why exact planting date matters

Rabi/Kharif is useful, but it is broad. HR Fram now stores the exact sowing or planting date so the final model can engineer planting-time and crop-age features.

Connected workflow

1Crop Recommendation suggests a crop.
2User selects Predict Yield for Best Match.
3Location, weather, soil and farm area come across automatically.
4User completes timing/management details and predicts yield.
